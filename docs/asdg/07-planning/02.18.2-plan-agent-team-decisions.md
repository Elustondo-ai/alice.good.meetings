## Open questions for review

1. **Word order.** Is `asdg-team-create` beside `asdg-create-team` acceptable, or should the new skill be `asdg-team-prepare`?
2. **Writer mark.** A `writer` field on each member slot, or one map of folder to slot in the team's rules?
3. **Prepared participants.** Is the local cache the right place to record them, given a chat identity means nothing on another machine?
4. **Default focus and attitude.** Pablo's proposal says a def may include them. The session policy keeps both in the conversation. This plan leaves them out.
5. **Who may define an agent.** Only ASDG Admin, as phase E assumes, or also a project for its own use?
6. **Phase F.** Do the two single-agent skills earn a place, or is documenting Focus and delegation enough?
7. **Order.** New agent defs (E) after the first trial, as planned, or before it?
