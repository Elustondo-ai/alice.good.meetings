# See a proposed answer for the Focus question

UC-05 gives Pablo something to glance at while he reflects on an interview question. When he confirms a Focus question, Alice shows a few short points beside it and supporting details in an area below, and replaces both whenever the Focus question changes.
This draft of 16 November 2026 refines the shared "Prepare brief answer cues" behavior of the [overall UML model](01-system-model.md) and builds on the [Focus and Live cycle](01.4-confirm-current-question.md). It serves the SPRINT-003 commitments S003-ANSWER, S003-DETAILS, S003-CONTEXT and S003-NOKEY.

## Actors and what must be in place

Pablo is the primary actor: he confirms questions, glances at the points and answers in his own words. The interviewer asks through the conversation. Alice prepares the answer, and Pablo does nothing beyond Confirm to request it.
Before the interview an **interview preparation package** is in place: his résumé and other documents as plain text or Markdown, up to 20,000 characters, in the Git-ignored folder `poc/context/interview/`. Its text and the Focus question are sent to OpenAI to prepare the answer; [Architecture](../05-high-level-design-and-architecture/README.md) states exactly what leaves the Mac. Other meeting types come later.

## Main flow

1. Pablo's session is listening and a question is in Live, as in UC-04.
2. He presses **Confirm question**. Focus shows the wording, the answer card shows that an answer is being prepared, and the details area is empty.
3. The points, at most three, and the details appear together, complete, in place and without motion or sound. They come from one request.
4. Pablo glances at the points while he reflects, then answers in his own words. The details are read on demand, not at a glance: they are there if the interviewer wants more.
5. The cue (GOOD, LAND IT, STOP), the timer, Focus and Live behave exactly as before. The answer never writes Focus or Live.
6. When Pablo confirms another question, or a rewording of this one, the old points and details disappear at once and the flow returns to step 2.

## Alternatives and failures

| Situation | Expected observation | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus changes while an answer is pending or shown, including a confirmed rewording | The earlier answer is removed at once; an answer that arrives for the earlier wording is never shown | Decided: "Yes, always replace" |
| No OpenAI key, or the service cannot be reached | The page loads, the timer and cue work, and Pablo sees on load, before pressing anything, that question recognition and proposed answers are unavailable. Live and the answer card both say so, and Start listening is disabled with that reason. No question can be captured, so nothing waits for one | Decided on 18 November 2026 (C2): the key is put in place, and this minimal page is the fallback when it is missing |
| The request fails or takes too long | The service tries once more on its own; then the card says there is no proposed answer for this question. There is no retry button, and the next Focus change tries afresh | Decided: a message and no retry button; one retry by the service, decided on 18 November 2026 (C6) |
| The answer arrives after Pablo has started speaking | It appears in place, silently, like any other answer; nothing draws his eye and the cue is unaffected | Decided |
| The session stops or disconnects | The answer stays with its Focus question, both marked last known, not live. An answer still pending is dropped, and a new session starts empty | Decided: the answer stays as last known; the rest follows the UC-04 rule |
| No preparation package is present | Alice still proposes general points from the question alone, labelled as general, and states nothing about Pablo | Decided |
| The package is over 20,000 characters, empty or unreadable | The package is not used and is never cut short. The points are general and the label says why: too large, empty or unreadable | Decided 18 November 2026 (correction round, A1) |
| The package holds nothing relevant to the question | Organizing points only; never invented strengths, events, actions or results | Existing rule in UC-01 and UC-03 |

## Observable outcome

Pablo can take in the points at a glance and still answer in his own words; the cue remains the strongest signal on the page.
The answer on screen always belongs to the Focus question on screen, and it states nothing about Pablo that the package does not contain.
The seven states the preview must show are named in [answer states](01.5.1-proposed-answer-states.md).

## Decisions, proposals and open questions

Decided by Pablo on 16 November 2026, as relayed by 00 Governance and recorded in Planning's [addendum](../07-planning/03.6.3-sprint-003-brief-addendum.md): short bullet points with a details area below, one model call for both; a prepared context, which for interviews is the preparation package; any change to the Focus question replaces the answer. The automatic trigger carries forward from sprint criterion 3.
On 16 and again on 18 November 2026 00 Governance put the stages' proposed defaults to him as lists and he chose "Accept all" (18 November, confirmed by him): at most three bullets; details read on demand; a late answer appears silently; a failed request shows a message with no retry button; general points without a package; the answer stays as last known after Stop; and the package's format, size and folder. Also decided on 18 November 2026: the key is put in place with the minimal no-key page as fallback (C2); the details area is acceptable as a second text area, read on demand and scrolling inside itself when long, with no collapse control (C17, criterion 8); no delay limit is set in advance, and the service retries once (C5, C6); the sprint record is to be amended to add S003-DETAILS and S003-CONTEXT, which he triggers himself. Where Clear live, Start and Stop listening and the manual cue buttons go is left to the layout preview (C9), not to these pages. He sees each again in the preview and the specification review and can change any then.
No delay limit is set in advance; the wait is observed in the live session (S003-LIVE). Long details scroll inside their area (C17, criterion 8). Open: the preview's proposal for the controls (C9).
