# SPRINT-006 draft: the pen field

Pablo wants a big field below everything to write in with the pen. In Safari on iPadOS, Apple Pencil handwriting in an ordinary editable text field is converted to text by Scribble, which Pablo switches on in Settings under Apple Pencil. That is Apple's documented behaviour; it has not been tried on his iPad Pro 2015, which supports the first-generation Pencil, so the first check is on the real device. A plain text area is therefore enough: no canvas and no handwriting code. It is part of the [specification](03.9-sprint-006-screen-specification.md).

## Rules

| ID | Rule | Ties to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TF-1 | Area G is one `textarea` labelled "Write a question" with a Send button of at least 56 × 56 px beside it. Text is at least 28 px, plain, with spellcheck off. | Pablo 22 Nov (1) |
| TF-2 | It is a standard editable element: no canvas, no `contenteditable` overlay, no custom recognition, so Scribble and the on-screen keyboard both work. Whether Scribble writes into it on his iPad is checked by hand (SH6). | iPadOS Scribble |
| TF-3 | **Draft default (Q1).** Send trims the text and sets it as the Question at once, the same as confirming a question: Focus is set, the candidate is cleared and the answer step starts as for a spoken one. The request carries `via` `typed`. | UC-04; SC-4 |
| TF-4 | Writing is deliberate, so it needs no second Confirm; speech still needs a pause and a Confirm or Space as today. Nothing is changed in the speech path. | SC-6 |
| TF-5 | Send does nothing with empty or blank text and shows why. A written question longer than the service limit for a Question is not sent and the field says so. | SC-1; 05 |
| TF-6 | After Send the field is emptied and loses focus, so the keys G, L, S, A, Space, Esc and 1 to 0 work again; no key acts while the field has focus (SC-10 already excludes text fields). | SC-10 |
| TF-7 | The page never focuses the field by itself, not on load, after a state change or after an answer, so the iPad keyboard never opens in the meeting unasked. | Purpose |
| TF-8 | Text typed but not sent stays through a new spoken candidate, a new answer, a reconnect and a turn of the iPad. Nothing in the field is sent anywhere before Send. | TL-7 |
| TF-9 | A sent question is handled like any other: it goes to the service and the reader as the Question, and is logged as any confirmed question with `via` `typed` (05 and 04 confirm the log fields). | ML-1; 05 |
| TF-10 | The field has no erase control of its own: Scribble's scratch-out and the keyboard erase. It adds no other button. | TB-9 |
