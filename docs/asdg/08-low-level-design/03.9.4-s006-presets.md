# SPRINT-006 draft: preparation presets

Pablo's picture is a car radio: about ten buttons for his top prepared questions, "Tell me about yourself" being button 1. A real radio makes you remember what each button is, so each button carries a one or two word label as a clue. Pressing one shows that prepared question and its answer without anyone saying the question. They come from an extended package of pre-computed questions and answers, whose format 05 and 03 settle. He uses them while preparing and while performing. It is part of the [specification](03.9-sprint-006-screen-specification.md).

## Rules

| ID | Rule | Ties to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TP-1 | **Changed (Pablo 22 Nov, labels).** Area F is ten buttons. Each shows a label of one or two words (for example "About me", "Conflict", "Biggest failure", "Why us") with a small number in its top-left corner, so the keys 1 to 9 and 0 stay discoverable. Button 1 is the first prepared question; Pablo's own choice is "Tell me about yourself", labelled "About me". | Pablo 22 Nov (3) |
| TP-2 | One tap sets the Question to that prepared question and shows its prepared bullets under "Alice suggests" at once. No speech, no Confirm and no model call. The heading says "Prepared" so it is not taken for a live answer. | TP-4 |
| TP-3 | A tap has the effect of a confirmation for Focus: it clears the candidate and starts the cue hold rules as before, and a preset tap is logged with `via` `preset` and its number. Nothing is played aloud. | SC-4, SA-16 |
| TP-4 | The prepared answer is bullets only: at most three bullets of at most 120 characters, as in AD-1 to AD-4. Details are not shown. | AD |
| TP-5 | The active preset is marked with a filled button and a thick outline. Tapping it again does nothing; tapping another replaces the content in place. Any other way of setting the Question (speech, written, Confirm) removes the mark. | CT-4 |
| TP-6 | An empty slot is a disabled button with its number and a dash and no label; the row never changes size. With no presets in the package, a line says "No prepared questions in the package". | SA-2 |
| TP-7 | Presets work not listening (preparing) and while listening, and in every state of the screen. | Pablo 22 Nov (3) |
| TP-8 | **Changed (S003 PG-13; Q8).** Package text must not reach the page, but a preset answer must. Proposed: the page receives only the ten titles at load; a tap sends the number and the service returns that one question and its bullets; the rest of the package never reaches the page. | PG-13; 05 |
| TP-9 | **Draft format, changed (labels; 05, 03 settle).** One file in the package lists up to 10 entries `{label, question, bullets}` in button order, produced outside the screen. `label` is one or two words of at most 16 characters; `question` is the full text shown as the Question. The screen has no editor and no reordering. | Pablo 22 Nov |
| TP-10 | **Changed (labels).** Text on a preset is at least 16 px bold for the label and 14 px for the number, never smaller. Buttons are at least 56 px high (72 px by TL-5) and at least 112 px wide. One row of ten fits at 1260 px page width or more (122 px each at 1366, 130 px at 1440, with 16 px margins and 12 px gaps). Below 1260 px wide, and in portrait, there are two rows of five, each at least 180 × 64 px. | TB-4, TL-5 |
| TP-11 | On a keyboard the keys 1 to 9 and 0 select presets 1 to 10, under the SC-10 conditions (focus not on a button or text field, no modifier). The page does not scroll. | SC-10; multimodal rule |
| TP-12 | The page makes no request except to its own service; the presets add no network target. | PG-13 |
| TP-13 | **New (labels).** A label wraps to at most two lines, one word per line when needed, and is never a third line: a longer one is cut with an ellipsis. A label over 16 characters in the package is shortened by the service to 15 plus an ellipsis and counted in the load result; a missing label is the first 16 characters of the question. Only the label may be cut (an exception to SA-2); the full question appears in B on a tap. | Pablo 22 Nov; SA-2 |
| TP-14 | A word of a label is at most 11 characters, so that it fits one line of a button 112 px wide at 16 px bold; a longer word is cut by TP-13. | TP-10 |
