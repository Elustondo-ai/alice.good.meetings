# SPRINT-006 draft: questions for Pablo and what 03, 05 and 04 must settle

Every default in rules TL, TF, TB and TP is a proposal until Pablo answers. This page lists the decisions and who else must settle what before a sprint can use them. It is part of the [specification](03.9-sprint-006-screen-specification.md).

## Questions for Pablo (recommendation first)

| Q | Question | Recommended | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | What does writing in the field do? | One step: Send sets the Question at once. Other choices: Send makes a candidate that he confirms; or the field is a private note that Alice never sees. | TF-3, TF-4 |
| Q2 | Does a preset tap show at once, or need a Confirm? | At once | TP-2 |
| Q3 | Is a prepared answer bullets only, or with details too? | Bullets only (three) | TP-4 |
| Q4 | Is the touch strip shown on touch screens only, or always? | Touch only | TB-1 |
| Q5 | Order below the columns: strip, presets, field; or presets pinned to the bottom? | Strip, presets, field | TL-1 |
| Q6 | Does the iPad listen itself, or only show and control a session on the laptop? | See below | TQ-1 |
| Q7 | Keep the iPad screen awake while the page is open? | Yes | TQ-2 |
| Q8 | May the one tapped preset's text reach the page, though package text does not today? | Yes, that one only | TP-8 |
| Q9 | Which ten questions, and who produces their answers? | Pablo picks; produced with the package | TP-9 |
| Q10 | After Send, is the field emptied? | Yes | TF-6 |
| Q11 | Do the keys 1 to 9 and 0 select presets on the laptop? | Yes | TP-11 |
| Q12 | Is 56 px the right minimum target for his Pencil and fingers? | Yes, try on the device | TB-4 |
| Q13 | Label and number on each preset: both, or label alone? Who writes the labels? | Both; Pablo writes them with the package | TP-1, TP-9 |

**TQ-1.** The service listens on 127.0.0.1 over plain HTTP. An iPad cannot reach that address, and Safari gives the microphone only to secure (HTTPS) pages, so an iPad opening the laptop over the network could show and control but not listen. The hosted mode of the latest commit is HTTPS. Pablo and 05 must say which setup he means. **TQ-2.** Safari 16.4 has a screen wake lock; to be checked on the device.
