# SPRINT-006 draft: layout, sizes and orientation

Areas A to D are the SPRINT-004 bar, Question, candidate line and two columns, unchanged. This page places the three new areas under them and fixes the sizes to test. It is part of the [specification](03.9-sprint-006-screen-specification.md). Sizes are CSS pixels; an iPad Pro 12.9 inch is 1366 × 1024 landscape and 1024 × 1366 portrait, and Safari's toolbar takes some height, which is to be measured on his iPad.

## Rules

| ID | Rule | Ties to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TL-1 | Top to bottom the page is: A bar, B Question, C candidate, D columns (all SA-1 to SA-19 as they are), then E touch strip, F presets, G write field. Nothing existing moves, shrinks or disappears; E, F and G are added below D. | Pablo 22 Nov; SC-7 |
| TL-2 | **Changed (SA-1).** SA-1 said nothing else is visible; from this sprint E, F and G are also visible, and nothing else is. | SA-1 |
| TL-3 | The three sizes to check are iPad landscape 1366 × 920, iPad portrait 1024 × 1260 (both with Safari's toolbar, to be confirmed on the device) and laptop 1440 × 800. | Pablo 22 Nov |
| TL-4 | At 1366 × 920 with a typical Question (two lines) and three bullets, areas A to G are all visible without scrolling. With the longest content the page may scroll below D as SA-2 allows; the bar stays sticky. | SA-2, SA-3; draft limit |
| TL-5 | Heights: E at least 64 px, F at least 72 px (one row of ten from 1260 px page width, two rows of five below that and in portrait, TP-10), G at least 160 px in landscape, 240 px in portrait, 120 px on the laptop. Gaps between buttons are at least 12 px. | draft limits, preview to fix |
| TL-6 | On the laptop (no touch screen) E is not shown; F and G are. | TB-1 |
| TL-7 | Turning the iPad re-lays the page out without a reload and keeps the Question, candidate, active preset and any text in the field. The page does not lock the orientation. | iPad use |
| TL-8 | The page respects the iPad's safe areas (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`) so no button sits under the Home indicator. Pinch zoom is not disabled. | iPad use |
| TL-9 | Colours, contrast (at least 4.5 to 1), fonts and the ban on animation, transition and sound are those of SPRINT-004. A pressed button changes colour at once and nothing else. | SA-16 |
| TL-10 | The new areas have the same plain-text rule as the rest: nothing from the service is shown as markup. | S003 PG-12 |
| TL-11 | Heights above are draft: 03 or the preview (as in SPRINT-003) fixes final values, and `iPad landscape` is checked by a viewport and touch emulation of that size. | 04 |
