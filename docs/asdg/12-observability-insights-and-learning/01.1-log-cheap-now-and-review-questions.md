# Meeting log: cheap now, hard later, and first review questions

Part of [what the log must let Pablo learn](01-meeting-log-learning-needs.md). Owner: 12 Observability. Draft of 19 November 2026; a proposal, nothing approved or built.

## Cheap now, hard later

1. **A schema version** on every file, and an append-only line-per-event format so a crash loses at most one event.
2. **The two clocks** above; adding a monotonic clock afterwards cannot be recovered.
3. **Event source and cause** on every cue and Focus change; "why did it say STOP" cannot be reconstructed later.
4. **Time limits and thresholds in force** (turn limit, cue rules) recorded in the header, so old sessions stay interpretable after tuning.
5. **Which text came from where**, as a label only, never package text. This keeps the "no package text" rule while still showing the answer's origin.
6. **Optional audio file reference** (a path or hash, not the audio) so a later review tool can align a recording if Pablo keeps one.
7. **Pablo's own mark**: one button or key that stamps "this moment" with a note slot. Cheap in the page, and impossible to recreate afterwards.

Not recommended now: sentiment or quality scores. Those are interpretation, added by review tooling later, never written into the raw record.

## (2) First review questions

1. How long was each speaking turn, and how often did a turn pass the STOP point?
2. After LAND IT or STOP appeared, how long until the turn ended?
3. How long did he pause before answering a new question, and how long were mid-answer pauses?
4. When a new Focus question appeared, what did he say next, and did it address that question?
5. How often did a proposed answer appear, how soon after the question, and did his next words resemble it?
6. Which cues came while he was already finishing, and which were late or wrong?
7. Where did Alice fail (no key, lag, errors), so those moments are excluded from judging him?

## Limits and hand-off

Questions 4 and 5 need judgment; the log supplies evidence, not a verdict. Retention, who may read the file and its location belong to 02 Purpose and 05 Architecture; the format belongs to 08 Detailed Design. No code, sprint state or approval was changed.
