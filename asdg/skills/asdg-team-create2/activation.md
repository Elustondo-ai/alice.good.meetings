# Activation prompts and titles

Use these instructions with [the session protocol](session-protocol.md); substitute actual verified values, never illustrative IDs.
Pass the explicit setup invocation and its limits, not invented human approval. Child chats do not inherit the caller's history.
Each chat follows canonical ASDG role instructions; no native TOML agent loading or tool-level enforcement is claimed.

## Coordinator prompt

Identify the verified absolute project root, chosen `Team N`, caller chat link and this setup run.
Explicitly assign `ASDG-COORDINATOR`; load the installed Focus workflow, client/bootstrap guidance and every required canonical Governance source.
Initialize NoFocus and NoAttitude; retain exact custom title `Team N` under the [title contract](../asdg-focus/rename-chat.md).
State: "The caller is creating and placing your twelve specialists. Do not create sessions, delegate setup or invoke team creation. You coordinate this team after setup; wait for the final roster."
Explain that the caller first promotes this new coordinator chat to an independent session; its initial owner link may change. Use native readback after promotion, not an old staging link, for subsequent routing.
Request a short receipt with native identity, observed title/project, actual canonical reads, role responsibilities/exclusions and activation confirmed or exact blockers.
No project tasks, file edits, installs, tests, branches, worktrees, sprints, commits or pushes. Acknowledge activation and await work, without generic onboarding questions.

## Specialist prompt

Identify the verified absolute project root, final top-level team link/title, exact role ID and custom title from the table below.
Explicitly assign that role via the installed `asdg-focus` adapter and canonical `asdg/skills/asdg-focus/SKILL.md`; supply their resolved absolute paths.
Supply the catalogue-selected role entry, contract, local procedure and TOML adapter path; require all mandatory startup and role sources, shared runtime, response envelope and documentation authority to be read in full.
TOML adapters are instruction evidence; do not claim the host loaded a native TOML agent.
Initialize NoFocus and NoAttitude; preserve the exact custom title, including spaces. Do not add a team number or project suffix to specialist titles.
State: "Your final coordinator is <team link>. You may temporarily appear under the caller while it moves this chat; do not treat that staging location as your coordinator or move yourself."
Require canonical-read evidence and a short acknowledgment of the assigned role, responsibilities, exclusions, project, native chat ID, observed title and any activation blocker.
Purpose may perform its required read-only opening/status, but must respect the explicit instruction to wait rather than start an interview or write artifacts.
Retain the activated role for later turns. Team membership and a move never widen that role's authority.
No project tasks, file edits, installs, tests, worktrees, branches, sprints, commits, pushes, further sessions or peer messages; acknowledge and await work.

## Final coordinator handoff

Send the verified team link, twelve role/current-link pairs, activation/placement status and any unresolved blockers.
Say that setup is finished or partial, that the caller's context is separate, and that no work is assigned; the coordinator must not duplicate the roster.
Request only acknowledgment and waiting. A rejected or pending message is not delivered context; preserve it in the caller's report for recovery.
